# Stock Management System

A simple and efficient stock management system designed for small businesses, retail shops, and personal inventory management. It helps track inventory, sales, suppliers, repair/service jobs, and business performance through an intuitive dashboard.

## ✨ Features

### 📊 Dashboard

* Business overview at a glance
* Total products
* Total suppliers
* Current stock quantity
* Inventory value
* Total sales
* Total profit
* Today's sales
* Monthly sales
* Low stock alerts
* High-selling products
* Recent stock entries
* Recent sales
* Recent repair/service records

---

## 🔐 User Authentication

* Secure login
* Protected dashboard and management pages

---

## 📦 Product Management

* Add, edit, and remove products
* Product image support
* Product description
* Purchase price
* Selling price
* Automatic stock tracking
* Low stock monitoring

---

## 🚚 Supplier Management

* Manage supplier information
* Contact details
* Address
* Notes
* Link suppliers to stock entries

---

## 📥 Stock Entry

Record incoming inventory with:

* Automatic date selection
* Product selection
* Quantity
* Purchase price per item
* Automatic total calculation
* Selling price
* Optional supplier
* Optional image
* Notes

---

## 💰 Sales Management

Create and manage sales with:

* Automatic date and time
* Quantity management
* Automatic price calculation
* Manual price adjustment when needed
* Multiple payment methods
* Customer information (optional)

  * Name
  * Phone Number
  * Address
* Sales history
* Automatic stock deduction

Supported payment methods:

* Cash
* eSewa
* Bank Transfer

---

## 🛠 Repair & Service Management

Manage repair and service jobs such as:

* Phone repair
* Tempered glass replacement
* Watch repair
* Electronic repairs
* Accessories repair
* Other custom services

Record:

* Service type
* Customer information (optional)
* Cost
* Charged amount
* Profit
* Payment method
* Notes
* Date and time
